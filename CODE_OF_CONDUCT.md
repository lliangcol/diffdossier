# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a harassment-free, inclusive project. Be respectful,
assume good intent while examining evidence, and focus criticism on behavior
and technical work.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory language,
publication of private information, or sustained disruption. Maintainers may
remove content or restrict participation to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately using the contact method in SECURITY.md.
Maintainers will handle reports confidentially and avoid conflicts of interest
where possible.
